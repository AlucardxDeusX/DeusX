Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Introduction

Control ("we", "our", or "the app") is a device diagnostics and testing application designed to help users test various hardware and functions of their Android devices.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Control may access, how that information is used, and how we protect your privacy while you use the application.

Information and Device Data

Control may access certain device information and hardware features when you use specific testing functions. Depending on the tests you perform, this may include:

  • Device model and manufacturer information
  • Android version and device software information
  • Available sensors and sensor readings
  • GPS/location information when you use the GPS test
  • Camera access when you use the camera test
  • Microphone access when you use the microphone test
  • Speaker and audio functionality when performing audio tests
  • Flashlight/torch functionality
  • Vibration functionality
  • Other device information required to perform diagnostics

The application only accesses hardware features when they are required for the corresponding test or functionality.

Location Information

The GPS test may request access to your device's location. Location information is used only to perform and display the GPS/location test within the application.

Control does not use your location for advertising or sell your location information to third parties.

Camera and Microphone

The camera is accessed only when you use the camera testing functionality. The microphone is accessed only when you use the microphone testing functionality.

These permissions can be managed or revoked through your Android device settings.

How We Use Information

Information and device features accessed by Control are used to:

  • Perform hardware and functionality tests
  • Display device and diagnostic information
  • Help identify whether supported device functions are working
  • Provide and improve the application's functionality

Data Collection and Storage

Control is designed to perform diagnostics directly on your device. We do not intentionally collect or sell personal information through the application.

If any information is stored locally on your device, it is used only to support the application's functionality.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Information may only be shared with third-party service providers if such sharing is necessary to provide a specific application service, comply with legal requirements, or protect the security and integrity of the application.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information accessed by the application against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Services

Control may use third-party services or libraries required for certain application functionality. Such services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Control is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.

Your Permissions

You can manage the permissions granted to Control through your Android device settings. Revoking a permission may prevent the corresponding diagnostic test from functioning.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
